# Salary research — 2026-09-27

**Purpose:** Sam asked me to help pick a target number because he doesn't have one in mind and
doesn't think of himself as entry level. This is the evidence. It is research, not a recommendation —
the recommendation is at the bottom.

## The honest framing of "not entry level"

Sam's instinct is right, and the market's instinct is also right, and they are not in conflict:

- **He is not entry level** in the sense that matters: he has done owned work, not shadowed it. He ran
  Intune at scale, wrote the provisioning automation, supported a multinational under SLA. That is a
  mid-level shape.
- **The market will read him as entry-junior** on paper, because of the 22-month gap. The gap doesn't
  erase the experience; it costs him the *first* 2–3 years of the pay curve. A recruiter pricing by
  "years of continuous employment" will anchor lower than the work justifies.

So the strategy is: **apply as mid-level, price as mid-level, and let the first offer set the floor
rather than bidding against yourself early.** Don't volunteer a number below the band in the first
exchange. If asked for expectations, quote the top of the band, not the bottom.

## Proposed target

| | target | stretch |
|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1 target** | **CA$58,000** | CA$65,000 |
| **Tier 2 target** (endpoint/Intune, bilingual) | **CA$75,000** | CA$90,000 |
| **Tier 2 security** | CA$68,000 | CA$75,000 |
| **Walk-away floor** | **CA$50,000** | below this, take contract work and keep searching |

**Why the floor is 50k:** below that, a relocation is not worth the housing cost even with parental
support covering the first months. It also signals a role that will teach nothing, which makes the gap
on the CV longer rather than shorter.

**Why 58k is the Tier 1 target and not 55k:** the Indeed average is 57,976. Bidding at the average is
the play — below it you're competing on price against people with continuous employment, which is the
one axis where you lose.

**The real ceiling is Tier 2, and it is reachable.** The CMHC posting is the proof: CA$87k for
endpoint + French. If the Tier 1 applications all land under 65k, that isn't a failure — it means the
applications are aimed at the wrong tier.

## Method caveats — read before trusting these numbers
1. **Aggregator salary data is self-reported and skewed** toward mid-career people who stayed. It
   systematically under-reports the entry band and over-reports the top.
2. **Provincial spread is real but these sources don't cleanly separate it.** The Ontario figures
   above are Ontario figures. Québec public sector follows its own grids, not these bands.
3. **Nothing here is verified against a live shortlist.** This is desk research for setting an
   anchor. The first three real offers are better data than everything on this page.
4. **Re-check before the CAF track** — public-sector security has its own classification and salary
   grid, and if CAF lands it supersedes all of this.
