## Titles to search

Not a fixed list — run these as search strings, and read postings for adjacent titles. Hiring managers
post to the title they know, not the technically-correct one.

### Tier 1 — apply first, most volume, fastest to a first salary
- IT Support Specialist / IT Support Technician
- Service Desk Analyst / Service Desk Technician (Tier 1 / L1)
- Helpdesk Analyst / Helpdesk Technician
- Desktop Support Technician / Desktop Support Engineer
- End User Computing (EUC) Analyst / EUC Technician
- Junior Systems Administrator / IT Support Administrator
- Technical Support Representative (Tier 1)

**Why these lead:** Simon Hegele + Rowse Honey map onto them almost one-to-one, and the PowerShell
automation plus multi-site Intune work is a differentiator against the generic helpdesk candidate.
They set the salary floor.

### Tier 2 — better ceiling, genuinely scarce skills, worth real attention
- **Intune Administrator / Endpoint Management Administrator / MDM Administrator** — the scarcest
  match on the CV. He administered Intune at scale, built the PowerShell provisioning automation,
  and separately ran an in-house MDM. Few candidates have all three.
- **Endpoint Engineer / Endpoint Management Analyst**
- **SOC Analyst Tier 1 / Junior Security Analyst / Cybersecurity Analyst (entry)** — Cisco Ethical
  Hacker + Junior Cybersecurity Analyst Career Path are the credentials; the PHANTOM pipeline is real
  applied work. Entry-level SOC postings exist and are the correct read on those certs.
- **IT support with French required** — Québec weights this heavily and he has the language.
- **Technical Support Engineer (hardware/software)** — slight upgrade on Tier 1 helpdesk, keeps the
  hardware-diagnostics angle alive.

### Tier 3 — opportunistic, don't over-invest
- Québec public sector IT (structured hiring, slower, stable — go through Québec Emploi)
- Contract / gig helpdesk for bridge income while Tier 1 runs
- IT generalist at a small MSP (broad scope, fastest route to a cheque)

### French equivalents — search these directly
`soutien informatique` · `technicien(ne) informatique` · `analyste du centre de services` ·
`administrateur système` · `gérant des accès` (Entra identity work) · `analyste sécurité` ·
`technicien(ne) soutien aux utilisateurs`

## Positioning by posting type

The split, from CV v7's real material:

- **Sysadmin / support / EUC postings** → lead with the Intune migration and the PowerShell
  automation, plus trilingual remote support under SLA. That is the pitch.
- **Security postings** → lead with the Cisco certs and the PHANTOM pipeline. A self-taught
  candidate with genuine applied security-research work is rarer than one with another helpdesk job.
- **Quebec public sector** → formal register, French-forward, certifications listed formally. The
  trilingual and no-sponsorship-required lines carry real weight there.
- **MSPs** → the broad-scope angle. Small-team autonomy reads as an advantage, not thin experience.
